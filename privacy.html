Privacy Policy

SupoClip Autopilot · Effective November 16, 2026

SupoClip Autopilot is a personal tool that runs entirely on its owner's computer and is used only by its owner to manage their own accounts.

What it accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube account, the app requests these Google permissions:

  • youtube.upload, to upload clips the owner has approved to the owner's own channel;
  • youtube.readonly, to read the channel's identity and the status and statistics of videos the app uploaded.
  • youtube.force-ssl, to set the owner's own channel description, keywords and banner, and to post and pin a comment on the owner's own uploads that points viewers to the same brand's other account.

For TikTok it requests user.info.basic, video.publish and video.list for the same purposes.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in a database on the owner's own computer. Video files, transcripts and publishing records also stay on that computer. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third-party server; the only network calls go to the platforms' own APIs to upload and check videos.

How data is used and shared

Data obtained from Google APIs is used only to provide the uploading and status features above. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. SupoClip Autopilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while an account is connected. Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its stored tokens and revokes the grant with the platform. Access can also be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions or TikTok's app settings.
