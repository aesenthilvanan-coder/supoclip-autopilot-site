SupoClip Autopilot

A personal, self-hosted tool. Not a public service.

SupoClip Autopilot runs on its owner's own computer. It turns long videos the owner has the rights to into short vertical clips with a self-hosted copy of the open-source SupoClip, and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts through the platforms' official APIs.

It is used only by its owner, for their own accounts. There is no sign-up and no other users.
